# mask2ai logo brief

## What the product is

mask2ai keeps personal data on your machine when you talk to an AI. It sits between you and the model: before a message, a file or a command result leaves the computer, it finds names, emails, phone numbers, card numbers, IBANs, national ID numbers, social security numbers, dates of birth and street addresses and replaces each with a placeholder. The model only ever sees placeholders. On the user's screen, in their files and in the commands that run, the real values are put back. Nothing is sent to any server of ours; there is no account.

It ships as a plugin for the Claude Code terminal and as a Chrome extension for claude.ai and ChatGPT. The audience is developers and privacy-conscious professionals.

## The idea to express

A quiet, local guard. Two sides of the same text: the real one that stays with you, the masked one that goes out. Substitution, not deletion. Nothing dramatic, nothing "hacker".

## Name

Wordmark reads `mask2ai`, all lowercase, one word. The "2" is "to". Do not style it as "mask 2 AI" or "Mask2AI".

## Visual direction

- Minimal, geometric, works at 16 px as a favicon and at 512 px.
- One idea, drawn plainly. Candidate motifs: a redaction bar over part of a line of text; a text line whose middle segment is swapped for a solid block; a simple mask shape made from two rectangles; a small shield only if it stays abstract.
- Monospace or geometric sans for the wordmark. The product's site uses JetBrains Mono.
- Palette: a single accent, purple `#6b21a8` on light backgrounds and `#c084fc` on dark, plus near-black `#1c1b18` and off-white `#fbfaf7`. The mark must work in one colour.
- Flat, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no padlock, no eye, no robot, no brain, no circuit board.
- Deliverables: mark alone, wordmark alone, mark plus wordmark horizontal; SVG; light and dark versions; a 32 px favicon variant.

## Reference feel

The restraint of brew.sh and the plainness of a terminal. If it looks like a security company or a crypto project, it is wrong.
